Leadership Development Focus

Two Development Tracks: Pike & Komodo Dragon

Strategy calls for structured, repeatable and visible development - not complex programmes. The detail below translates the source behaviours into a practical first version.

Source + structured detail
Track 1

BAR → SSV Development Series (Pike Series)

Build confidence in SSV support behaviours before promotion
  • Identify: initiative, team influence, customer focus and coachability
  • Expose: training, standards, deployment awareness and leadership communication
  • Practise: trainer work, shift support, improvement ideas and role-model behaviour
  • Coach: judgement, communication, standards ownership and confidence supporting the team
  • Validate: readiness through observed behaviours, not aspiration alone
Evidence for Readiness to Compete:

Can support a SSV, reinforce routines & standards appropriately, communicate clearly with partners and customers, and follow through on an assigned leadership responsibility.

Track 2

SSV -> Coffeehouse Coach Development Series (Komodo Dragon Series)

Develop beyond SSV execution into people leadership and strategic ownership
  • Develop: coaching, delegation, resilience and business thinking
  • Expose: staffing, planning, follow-up and partner development
  • Practise: store meetings, planning work, mentoring and cross-store exposure
  • Own: a defined store opportunity with measurable follow-through
  • Validate: ability to lead through others, not simply execute personally
Evidence for Readiness to Compete:

Can coach and delegate consistently, translate priorities into partner action, use basic business information to make decisions, and follow an initiative from planning through validation.

Development Effectiveness Check: promotion should be the result of demonstrated readiness. Each series creates experiences that let partners practise next-level behaviours before a role becomes available.
Building a culture of coaching

Separating Feedback From Development

Leaders should understand the difference between coaching and feedback. (insert quote here)

Source-backed quote

Feedback

  • Often reactive
  • Clarifies what happened
  • Corrects or reinforces a specific behaviour
  • Can be short and immediate

Coaching

  • Future-focused and developmental
  • Builds capability over time
  • Uses questions, follow-up and reflection
  • Builds confidence and judgement
D133 coaching standard

Clear. Concise. Consistent. Supportive. Set the expectation, help the partner understand the why, follow up to validate, and recognise progress.

1. Clarify

What behaviour or capability are we developing, and why does it matter?

2. Explore

Ask questions that surface judgement, barriers and the partner's own thinking.

3. Commit

Agree one observable next action or practice opportunity.

4. Validate

Return to it. Observe the behaviour, recognise progress and adjust the next step.

Proposed operating detail

30/60-day Launch Target

Adapt this version so that it is simple enough to execute, visible enough to share, and creates the right accountability and structure necessary for sustained change.

Draft Proposal

Days 1-15 | Align

  • Confirm BAR → SSV → Coffeehouse Coach as the first district pipeline priority.
  • Define a small set of observable readiness behaviours for each transition.
  • Set the minimum PDP expectation and monthly follow-up rhythm.
  • Each coffeehouse identifies active candidates where possible.
  • Create a simple district talent snapshot: emerging, ready-next, ready-now.

Days 15-30 | Activate

  • Begin monthly development conversations and documented next actions.
  • Assign stretch experiences tied to readiness gaps.
  • Calibrate leaders on feedback vs developmental coaching.
  • Use mentoring, trainer work and selected cross-store exposure.
  • Focus: Onboarding, MyLearning and Skill-check follow-through.

Days 30-45/60 | Validate

  • Run the first quarterly talent review.
  • Compare pipeline strength and gaps across stores.
  • Validate readiness with observed evidence and progress.
  • Identify stalled development actions and reset ownership.
  • Agree what to scale, simplify or change for the next 90 days.

Leading indicators

Are We Doing The Work?

  • Monthly development conversation completion
  • Active PDPs with a current next action
  • MyLearning completion and onboarding quality
  • Skill-check and coaching follow-through
  • Stretch assignments and cross-store experiences in progress
Lagging indicators

Is The Work Creating A Bench?

  • Internal promotion rate
  • Ready-now / ready-next bench by role
  • Stores with persistent pipeline gaps
  • Retention of identified talent
  • Time to fill leadership openings internally

Suggested Quarterly Talent Snapshot

Emerging

Shows potential; focus is exposure, fundamentals and consistent performance.

Ready Next

Demonstrates several next-level behaviours; has specific gaps and active stretch work.

Ready Now

Consistently demonstrates required behaviours and can be considered when an opportunity exists.

Meeting-ready

Talking Points:

Proposed wording
D133 doesn't have a talent problem. We need a talent operating system. The opportunity is to make development consistent enough that every coffeehouse can identify who is growing, what they are growing toward and what experience they need next.
Centre version one on BAR → SSV → Coffeehouse Coach. PDPs, coaching, training and cross-store exposure are not separate initiatives - they're the mechanisms that move people through the pipeline.
The strategy has to be able to allow us to answer 3 questions at any given time: who is emerging, who is getting ready, and what does each person need next?
At 90 days, the first proof is not just promotions. It's whether every leader can see their bench more clearly, whether development actions are actually happening, and whether we know where the district's gaps are and who might be next in line to fill them.
Close with a decision

Can we align on BAR → SSV → Coffeehouse Coach as the first priority and pilot a simple 30-45-60-90-day operating rhythm around PDPs, coaching, readiness to compete and total district talent visibility?
